# TEST — Village Ebook\n\n# STARR Learn • Play • Grow\n\n## Product purpose\nA practical, original STARR learning guide designed to be useful, printable and ready for human QC. This is a production artifact created because the normal AI provider is temporarily rate-limited.\n\n## 1. Start Here\nSet one clear goal for the learner. Write the goal at the top of the page, gather only the materials needed, and decide how success will be checked.\n\n## 2. Learn\nRead the short explanation for the topic, then identify three facts you can verify from a reliable source. Separate observed facts from guesses.\n\n## 3. Mission\nComplete one practical task. Record what you used, what you did, what happened and what you would change next time.\n\n## 4. Activity\nCreate a simple hands-on activity using ordinary household, garden or classroom materials where appropriate. Work slowly, keep the workspace tidy and record the result.\n\n## 5. Challenge\nChange one variable and repeat the activity. Compare the two results and explain the difference in your own words.\n\n## 6. Worksheet\nGoal: ____________________\nMaterials: ____________________\nWhat I predicted: ____________________\nWhat happened: ____________________\nWhat I learned: ____________________\nWhat I will try next: ____________________\n\n## 7. Family Mission\nTeach one thing from this guide to another person. Ask them one question and record their answer.\n\n## 8. Practical Project\nPlan a small real-world project. List the steps, materials, time needed, safety checks and evidence that the project worked.\n\n## 9. Reflection\nWhat was easiest? What was hardest? What surprised you? What would you improve?\n\n## 10. STARR Achievement\nAward yourself a STARR progress mark when the learner has completed the activity, recorded evidence and explained what was learned.\n\n## Safety and quality\nUse age-appropriate supervision. Follow instructions for tools, heat, animals, food, plants and outdoor activities. Do not eat or handle anything unidentified. A final human QC pass is required before commercial release.\n\n## Production checklist\n- Original STARR wording and structure checked\n- Learning objective present\n- Practical activity present\n- Worksheet present\n- Safety section present\n- Sources/evidence to be added and checked during QC\n- Artwork/layout to be added during Design\n- Final proof and marketplace listing pack required\n\n**STARR — Learn • Play • Grow**\n
