# STARR Village Test E-book

## The Little Homestead Mission

A practical family learning mini-guide from STARR.

### Mission 1 — Observe
Look around your home or garden. Find three living things and three non-living things. Draw them and write one sentence about each.

### Mission 2 — Grow
Choose one easy-to-grow food plant. Record the seed, soil, water and sunlight it needs. Check it every day for seven days.

### Mission 3 — Measure
Measure the plant on day 1 and day 7. Write the difference. What helped it grow?

### Mission 4 — Make
Design a tiny garden using recycled containers. Label where the water, soil and sunlight will go.

### Mission 5 — Think
What could your family grow to save money or produce fresh food? Give three ideas and explain one.

## STARR Challenge
Create your own one-page homestead mission and teach it to someone else.

## Safety
Use adult supervision for tools, sharp objects, heat, unfamiliar plants, animals and outdoor activities. Never eat an unknown plant.
