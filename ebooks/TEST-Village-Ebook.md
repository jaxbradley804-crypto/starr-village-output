# STARR Village Test E-book\n\n## The Little Homestead Mission\n\nA practical family learning mini-guide from STARR.\n\n### Mission 1 — Observe\nFind three living things and three non-living things. Draw them and write one sentence about each.\n\n### Mission 2 — Grow\nChoose one food plant. Record the seed, soil, water and sunlight it needs. Check it for seven days.\n\n### Mission 3 — Measure\nMeasure the plant on day 1 and day 7. Write the difference and explain what may have helped it grow.\n\n### Mission 4 — Make\nDesign a tiny garden using recycled containers. Label soil, water and sunlight.\n\n### Mission 5 — Think\nList three foods your family could grow and explain one choice.\n\n## STARR Challenge\nCreate your own one-page homestead mission and teach it to someone else.\n\n## Safety\nUse adult supervision for tools, heat, unfamiliar plants and animals. Never eat an unknown plant.\n\n**STARR — Learn • Play • Grow**\n
