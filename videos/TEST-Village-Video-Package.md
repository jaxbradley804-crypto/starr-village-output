# STARR Village Test Video Package

## Title
The Little Homestead Mission

## Format
Short family learning video / YouTube test

## Scene 1
Father and child arrive at the garden.
Narration: “Today we have a STARR mission — find out what plants need to grow.”

## Scene 2
Show soil, water and sunlight.
Narration: “Plants need the right combination of things to grow well.”

## Scene 3
Plant a seed and label it.
Narration: “Our job is to observe, care and learn.”

## Scene 4
Measure the plant and compare the result.
Narration: “Science is noticing what changes and asking why.”

## Scene 5
STARR Challenge card: “Design your own tiny garden.”

## End
“Learn. Play. Grow. See you on the next STARR mission!”
