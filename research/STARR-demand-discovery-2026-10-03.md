Canonical Brain artifact: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qJ5zW9JChx-v8mfcj7sArTZcfBz4tEiF0M0GDOeWTZw/edit

## Verified demand signals
- UK small-business AI: skills and unclear use cases remain major adoption barriers; practical content/admin/data/automation uses dominate.
- AI governance: integration and formal guidance remain shallow, creating a need for simple safe-use rules.
- Family AI literacy: Ofcom 2026 reports widespread AI use among children and material over-trust in AI-generated summaries.
- Small-space growing: RHS reports very large UK gardening participation and predicts continued tabletop/small-space food-growing interest.
- Community growing: strong participation/interest with practical funding, volunteer and administration barriers.

## STARR decisions
1. Test practical AI first-mile implementation for micro/small businesses.
2. Prototype a character-led Family AI Detective experience.
3. Extend existing STARR Grow Anywhere rather than creating another planner app.
4. Test a Community Grow Operations Kit.
5. Do not create a duplicate AI Policy Generator; verify/reuse Enterprise Project 140 if real.
6. Do not inflate the catalogue with near-identical static printables.
