# STARR SYSTEM MASTER PLAN

## Permanent operating rule
STARR is one integrated ecosystem. The AI Village should perform every safe routine task it can without Owner intervention. The Owner primarily handles genuine payment, account ownership/security, legal, platform authorization, withdrawals/transfers, and other actions that legally or technically require the Owner.

If an action genuinely requires the Owner, create one precise **OWNER ACTION REQUIRED** item stating exactly what must be done, then return all remaining work to the Village.

Never claim a connection, publication, payment route, integration, or sync is live without evidence.

## Village responsibilities
The Village owns routine research, planning, creation, testing, QC, fact checking, safety/compliance checks, fixes, retesting, packaging, assets, listings, descriptions, pricing research, SEO, metadata, catalog organization, bundles, marketing assets, launch preparation, sales tracking, support workflows, website/app product work, maintenance, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

E-products must be tested and QC checked by the Village; the Owner is not the product tester.

## Master Data System
Maintain one source of truth for products, videos, agents, tasks, decisions, integrations, versions, evidence, finances, blockers, releases and owner actions. Preserve history; never silently overwrite decisions or status.

## Agent memory and handover
Every agent records: task, input, work completed, output, evidence, changes, blockers, next action and receiving agent. Work is not considered handed over until the receiving system confirms receipt/result.

## Master task queue
The Village maintains and prioritizes the next safe actionable work automatically, minimizing Owner prompts and duplicated effort.

## Self-healing
Failed jobs are detected, safely retried where appropriate, logged, and escalated only when human intervention is genuinely required. Destructive or irreversible actions require the correct approval gate.

## Evidence-based status
Every important status such as LIVE, DONE, TESTING, BLOCKED or OWNER ACTION REQUIRED must have current evidence/timestamp where practical. Do not infer success from intention.

## Security and permissions
Use least privilege. Keep payment credentials, sensitive account actions, legal authorization and security-sensitive Owner controls behind the Owner boundary. Never place credentials in Brain notes, prompts, source code, logs or public repositories.

## Backup and recovery
Back up important Brain, dashboard, code, configuration, product and operational records. Maintain recovery/version information and a rollback path for important releases.

## Emergency stop and rollback
Maintain the ability to stop unsafe or malfunctioning automation and roll back supported releases/changes. Log the reason and recovery action.

## Revenue command centre
Track actual orders, customers, gross revenue, fees, net contribution, products, conversion data, payment status and progress toward the STARR financial targets. Distinguish verified actuals from targets/estimates.

## Customer/support loop
Capture customer questions, refunds, product problems, feedback and recurring issues as Village tasks. Feed verified lessons back into product and support improvements.

## Version control and traceability
Important products, app/site releases, Brain decisions, workflows and system changes must be versioned and traceable to their source/change.

## Expansion rule
STARR architecture must remain portable and modular so new AI tools, agents, platforms, shops, apps and future businesses can be added without rebuilding the whole system.
